Tire Services & Alignment

Tire rotation, balancing, and alignment to maximize tire life and handling.

Schedule this service

Overview

Proper tire care is essential for safety, fuel economy, and ride comfort. We offer tire rotation, balancing, and wheel alignment services. Our technicians use precision equipment to ensure your tires wear evenly and your vehicle handles correctly. We also provide tire repair and replacement recommendations.

What's included

  • Extended tire life and even wear
  • Improved handling and stability
  • Better fuel efficiency
  • Smoother, quieter ride

How it works

1

Tire Inspection & Pressure Check

We inspect tread depth, sidewall condition, and adjust pressure to manufacturer specs.

2

Rotation & Balancing

We rotate tires according to pattern and balance each wheel to eliminate vibration.

3

Precision Alignment

Using a laser alignment machine, we adjust camber, caster, and toe to factory specifications.

Common questions

How often should I rotate my tires?

Every 5,000 to 7,500 miles, or as recommended in your owner's manual. We include rotation with oil changes.

What are signs of alignment issues?

Pulling to one side, uneven tire wear, a crooked steering wheel, or vibration. If you notice any, schedule an alignment.
