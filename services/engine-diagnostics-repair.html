Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Accurate diagnostics and expert repairs for check engine lights and performance issues.

Schedule this service

Overview

When your check engine light comes on or your car isn't performing as it should, trust our diagnostic expertise. We use advanced scanners and experience to pinpoint the root cause—whether it's a sensor, fuel system, ignition, or mechanical issue. We then provide a clear plan for repair using quality parts.

What's included

  • Accurate diagnosis saves time and money
  • Restored engine performance and fuel efficiency
  • Prevention of minor issues becoming major repairs
  • Warranty-backed repairs for peace of mind

How it works

1

Computer Scan & Analysis

We connect diagnostic tools to read error codes and live data from your engine.

2

In-Depth Testing

We perform additional tests (compression, vacuum, fuel pressure) to confirm the issue.

3

Repair & Verification

We fix the problem and test drive to ensure the repair resolves the issue completely.
