Brake Repair & Replacement

Expert brake services to ensure your safety on the road.

Schedule this service

Overview

Your brakes are the most critical safety component of your vehicle. We inspect, repair, and replace brake pads, rotors, calipers, and lines using high-quality parts. Our technicians ensure your braking system performs optimally.

What's included

  • Enhanced safety and stopping power
  • Reduced noise and vibration
  • Longer lifespan for brake components
  • Peace of mind with thorough inspections

How it works

1

Inspection

We perform a comprehensive brake inspection, measuring pad thickness, rotor condition, and fluid levels.

2

Repair or Replace

Based on findings, we replace worn parts with OEM-grade components and machine rotors if needed.

3

Test Drive

We test drive your vehicle to ensure brakes are responsive and quiet before returning it to you.

Common questions

How often should I replace my brake pads?

Typically every 30,000 to 70,000 miles depending on driving habits. We recommend an inspection every 10,000 miles.

What are signs of brake problems?

Squealing, grinding, vibration, or a spongy brake pedal. If you notice any of these, bring your car in immediately.
