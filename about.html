Our story

About Dallas Auto Service

To provide honest, high-quality auto repair in Dallas, TX, with a focus on customer satisfaction and transparency.

Founded in 1995, Dallas Auto Service started as a small garage with a big mission: to offer reliable auto repair without the dealer price tag. Our founder, a lifelong mechanic, built the business on a foundation of trust and technical expertise. Over the years, we've grown into a full-service repair shop serving thousands of customers across Dallas.

Today, our team of ASE-certified technicians continues that legacy. We invest in the latest diagnostic tools and ongoing training to service all makes and models. Whether it's a simple oil change or a complex engine rebuild, we treat every car as if it were our own. Our 4.9-star rating and countless referrals are a testament to our commitment.
